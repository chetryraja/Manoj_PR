### Breakdown of Competitor Price Traps

1. **Mandatory Onboarding Extortion:**
   - *The Reality:* HubSpot mandates onboarding fees (\$1,500 on Sales Pro, \$3,500 on Sales Enterprise, \$3,000–\$7,000 on Marketing Hub). Enterprise ERPs (Dynamics/Salesforce) typically require consulting engagements equal to 1–2x the annual software license.
   - *Our Position:* **\$0 Mandatory Onboarding.** Self-guided interactive migration wizards, free automated CSV import mapping, and optional paid white-glove onboarding for high-complexity clients.
2. **The "Contact Tier" Tax:**
   - *The Reality:* Marketing CRMs charge for every contact stored in the database—even dormant, unmarketable, or unsubscribed leads.
3. **The Multi-Product Squeeze (Quoting & CPQ):**
   - *The Reality:* Sending quotes requires paying HubSpot Revenue Hub fees, purchasing Pipedrive Smart Docs, or paying \$105/user/month for Salesforce CPQ plus DocuSign.
